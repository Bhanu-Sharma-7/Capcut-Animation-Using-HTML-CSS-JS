1. Introduction

Welcome to Sudoku Offline Puzzle ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Bhanu Sharma. We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. By downloading, installing, or playing the Application, you agree to the collection and handling of information strictly in accordance with this policy.

2. Offline Processing & Zero First-Party Data Collection

100% On-Device Game Processing: Sudoku Offline Puzzle is designed to operate completely offline. The core puzzle generation engine (backtracking algorithm), board solver, pencil notes system, completion timer, and career statistics are computed exclusively on your device.

We do not operate any private backends, databases, or user accounts. Specifically:

  • We do not collect or store your personal name, email address, phone number, contacts, or real identity.
  • Your chosen in-game player name, avatar badge, total games won, win streak, personal best solve times, and ongoing board state are saved strictly in your device's sandboxed local storage (via AsyncStorage) and never leave your device to any proprietary server.
  • No account creation, sign-in, or registration is required to enjoy all features of the game.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the ongoing free development and maintenance of Sudoku Offline Puzzle, the Application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). AdMob delivers optional rewarded video ads (e.g., to voluntarily reveal an in-game hint) and periodic interstitial sponsor ads between games.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect, process, and share specific pseudonymous and device-level information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes. This data is handled in encrypted transit (HTTPS/TLS) and may include:

Data Category Data Elements Purpose
Device Identifiers Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, OS version, device model Ad delivery, impression measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate Location Coarse location derived from IP address Serving region-appropriate sponsor advertisements
App & Ad Diagnostics Ad views, click interactions, crash logs, performance metrics SDK stability, troubleshooting, and ad delivery analytics

4. Android Permissions Requested

The Application requests only the minimal technical permissions required for ad delivery and tactile polish:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the Google Mobile Ads SDK to fetch and display sponsor ads when an active internet connection is present.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables the ad service to detect whether the device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data before making ad requests.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Required on Android 13+ (API level 33+) by Google Play Services to access the device's advertising identifier for ad attribution.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback during number placement, errors, and puzzle completion.

6. User Rights & How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads

You have full control over advertising personalization and tracking on your Android device. You can opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Google Advertising ID at any time:

  • Open your device's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Tap "Reset advertising ID" to generate a fresh anonymous identifier, or select "Delete advertising ID" to opt out of interest-based ads entirely.

Additionally, you can play Sudoku Offline Puzzle completely offline (e.g., in Airplane Mode). When offline, the app operates without connecting to ad servers, while granting uninterrupted access to all difficulty tiers and game mechanics.
